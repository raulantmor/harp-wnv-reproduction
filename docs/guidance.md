# Applied research and writing guidance

Read on 2026-10-08:

- [Requested AGENTS.md](https://github.com/bradlipovsky/vdv-damage/blob/main/AGENTS.md)
- [Requested academic style guide](https://github.com/bradlipovsky/vdv-damage/blob/main/brad-lipovsky-academic-style-guide.md)

Applied principles: small research functions and plain tables; explicit
assumptions and units; equations connected to observed quantities; quantitative
comparisons; clear separation of observations, calculations, and inference;
self-contained figure captions; and bounded conclusions that identify failed
attempts. Content specific to the other project is not applied. This repository
uses scripts rather than notebooks, so its notebook-output instruction does not
add a notebook requirement. The user's independence and environment constraints
take precedence throughout.

Downloaded guidance copies remain in the ignored raw reference cache. They are
not redistributed as this project's original writing.
